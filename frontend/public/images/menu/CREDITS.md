## Notes on brand-name items

For brand-name drinks (Gatorade, SoBe, Tropicana) and the Beyond Burger patty, generic equivalents were used instead of branded product photos, per the sourcing guidelines for this menu:

- `lemon-lime-gatorade.jpg` — a glass of limeade (green sports-drink equivalent)
- `orange-gatorade.jpg` — a glass of orangeade (orange sports-drink equivalent)
- `sobe-yumberry-pomegranate.jpg` — a glass of pomegranate juice
- `tropicana-lemonade.jpg` — a cup of fresh lemonade
- `beyond-burger-patty.jpg` — a generic plant-based burger patty

All images were resized to an 800px bounding box with `sips -Z 800`.
